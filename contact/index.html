FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with clients outside the Philippines?

Yes. My clients are in the US, Canada, Australia and Europe, and I overlap with US business hours for calls and launches.

What does a typical project look like?

Audit or discovery call → written scope with architecture and timeline → staged build tested on real data → SOPs and a walkthrough. You see progress throughout.

Can you work inside my existing GHL account or agency?

Yes. I build in your sub-account or your agency's snapshot, follow your naming conventions, and can work white-label for agencies serving their own clients.

How do you charge?

Fixed price for scoped builds and audits, and a monthly retainer for ongoing systems work. You get a clear number before anything starts.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Most projects include a post-launch support window. The Systems Partner retainer covers monitoring, fixes and new workflows as your business grows.

Not sure what you need?

Start with a GHL Systems Audit. I review your account, workflows and integrations, find where leads, payments or follow-ups slip through, and give you a prioritised fix-first roadmap.
